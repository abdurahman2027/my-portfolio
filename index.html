About Me

I am a Statistics and Data Science student at Parami University, developing expertise in statistical analysis, data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. My academic work combines quantitative methods, programming, and practical problem-solving to address real-world challenges.

Alongside my studies, I have gained practical experience in data management, community coordination, digital literacy, education, and content development. I have also developed machine learning and data science projects involving predictive modeling, classification, clustering, NLP, time-series forecasting, and data visualization.

I am interested in the intersection of data, technology, education, and community development, with a focus on turning technical knowledge into practical and accessible solutions. I aim to continue growing as a data and technology professional while contributing to meaningful real-world impact.
